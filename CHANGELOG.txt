  - Only tokens with assigned initiative participate in turn stepping; the controls wrap at either end.
  - During a player character's active turn, that player may move their character and all NPCs delegated to them.
  - During a delegated NPC's active turn, its controlling player may move that NPC only.
  - During another player's turn or a GM-only NPC's turn, the player cannot move tokens; GM movement remains unrestricted at all times.
  - Clearing all initiative disables enforcement to avoid leaving player movement unintentionally locked.
  - Bumped persisted state schema to version 14. Existing installs default initiative enforcement to off.
